## 2. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to define the vision, objectives, stakeholders, scope, and long-term direction of the Aynal Haque Enterprise Portfolio project. This document serves as the foundation for all architectural, design, and development decisions throughout the software development lifecycle.
## 3. Product Vision
Aynal Haque Enterprise Portfolio is an enterprise-grade web application designed to showcase professional experience, software engineering skills, technical projects, articles, and services through a secure, scalable, and maintainable platform.

The platform enables recruiters, employers, clients, developers, and students to evaluate my professional capabilities while demonstrating modern software engineering practices.
## 4. Problem Statement
Currently, recruiters, employers, and clients must visit multiple platforms such as GitHub, LinkedIn, resumes, and social media to understand my professional profile.

This fragmented experience makes it difficult to evaluate my technical expertise efficiently.

## 5. Solution Statement
The portfolio provides a centralized platform where visitors can explore professional experience, projects, technical blogs, services, achievements, and contact information from a single location.

## 6. Mission
Build an enterprise-grade portfolio platform that demonstrates professional software engineering skills and creates opportunities for global employment and collaboration.

## 7. Target Users

### Recruiters

### Employers

Assess software engineering capability before hiring.

### Clients
